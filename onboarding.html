Your choice comes first

Before Hushfern starts

Hushfern needs permission to read visible webpage text so it can identify and blur potentially toxic content on your device.

01

What it reads

Visible text on regular HTTP and HTTPS webpages while you browse.

02

What stays on your device

The toxicity model, your settings, site-level counts, and any exceptions you deliberately save.

03

What it does not send

Page text is not uploaded to Hushfern or a remote moderation service.

By enabling protection, you allow Hushfern to inspect visible page text for on-device toxicity analysis. You can turn it off at any time.
